Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Covert Mask is an internal automation tool built and operated by Masar Stores (Barka, Oman). It is used exclusively to schedule and publish content that we create ourselves to our own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not a public product, and it is not made available to external users, customers, or third parties.

What data we handle

Because Covert Mask has no external users, the data it processes is limited to:

  • Our own content — video and image files, captions, and post metadata that we author for our own social channels.
  • Authentication tokens — OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok's official API, used solely to authorize publishing to our own connected TikTok account.

We do not collect, request, store, or process personal data belonging to any third party, customer, or end user. No public sign-up, login, or data-submission flow exists in this tool.

How we use this data

Authentication tokens are used only to call TikTok's official Content Posting API on our own behalf, in order to upload and publish our own video content. Tokens are stored securely within our internal automation environment and are never shared with, or accessible to, any third party.

Data retention

Authentication tokens are retained only for as long as needed to keep our publishing workflow functional, and are revoked or replaced whenever we rotate credentials. Content files are retained internally per our normal business record-keeping practices.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only external service this tool communicates with is TikTok's official Developer API, strictly for the purpose of publishing our own content.
